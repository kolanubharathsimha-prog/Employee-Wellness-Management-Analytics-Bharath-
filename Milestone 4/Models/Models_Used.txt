AI MODELS USED — Employee Wellness Management & Analytics
============================================================

1. langdetect
   Detects the language of a journal entry.

2. Google Translate (deep-translator)
   Translates non-English entries to English for analysis.

3. spaCy — xx_sent_ud_sm
   Multilingual sentence splitting, tokenization, lemmatization.

4. VADER (vaderSentiment)
   Rule-based sentiment scoring: Positive / Negative / Neutral.

5. BERT — bhadresh-savani/bert-base-go-emotion
   Transformer emotion classifier; mapped to the app's 6 emotions
   (Happy, Sad, Stress, Angry, Fear, Neutral).

6. Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct (local)
   Powers the Wellness Chat conversational assistant.

7. GPT-OSS-20B (via Groq API)
   Generates personalized journal recommendations (breathing/
   journaling/resource suggestions).

8. DeepFace
   Facial emotion recognition for the Live Face Scanner feature.
